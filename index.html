Kinetic Dash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Kinetic Dash is a game you can play with touch or with your body in front of your phone's camera. We built it so that nothing about you leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Kinetic Dash does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

  • No account, no login. You never give us a name, email or phone number.
  • No internet connection. The app does not ask for permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are in the app.

The camera (body mode)

  • If you choose Play with Body, the app asks for permission to use the front camera.
  • The camera picture is analysed on your phone, in real time, to find the positions of your head, shoulders and hips, so the game can tell when you jump, duck, lean or step.
  • The video is never recorded, saved or sent. Each frame is discarded right after it is analysed.
  • The app does not use the microphone.
  • You can play with touch instead and never allow the camera.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your phone only, so the game remembers them next time:

  • your best scores (one for touch, one for body mode);
  • your settings (music and sound volume, whether to show the camera picture);
  • which intro screens you have already seen.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Children

Kinetic Dash is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional features), we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
