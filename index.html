Kinetic Dash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Kinetic Dash is a game you can play with touch or with your body in front of your phone's camera. Your camera video never leaves your phone. The free version shows ads, provided by Google AdMob; a one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns them off.

The camera (body mode)

  • If you choose Play with Body, the app asks for permission to use the front camera.
  • The camera picture is analysed on your phone, in real time, to find the positions of your head, shoulders and hips, so the game can tell when you jump, duck, lean or step.
  • The video is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Each frame is discarded right after it is analysed. Neither the camera picture nor your body positions are ever given to the advertising tools or to anyone else.
  • The app does not use the microphone.
  • You can play with touch instead and never allow the camera.

What we (the developer) collect

Nothing. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics. You never give us a name, email or phone number, and we never see your scores, your camera or your purchases' payment details.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads: a small banner on the results screen after a run, and sometimes a full-screen picture ad when you leave the results screen (at most one every few minutes). Ads never appear during a run and never play sound.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app sends information to Google (not to us), such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and basic device information (model, Android version, language);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (such as the country) can be worked out;
  • which ads were shown and tapped, and diagnostic information about the ads.

How Google uses this is explained here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices:

  • Europe (EEA), the UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • US states with privacy laws (e.g. California): you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information in the same Privacy options.
  • On any Android phone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's Settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

"Remove ads" purchase

  • The one-time Remove ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.
  • The app only learns from Google Play whether this Google account has bought Remove ads, so it can switch the ads off, also on a new phone ("Restore purchase").
  • After you buy it, the app stops showing ads and no longer starts the advertising software at all.

Internet

The app uses the internet only for ads and for Google Play purchases. The game itself works offline (without ads).

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your phone only, so the game remembers them next time:

  • your best scores (one for touch, one for body mode);
  • your settings (music and sound volume, whether to show the camera picture);
  • which intro screens you have already seen;
  • whether ads are removed.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. (Google keeps the record of a purchase with your Google account.)

Children

Kinetic Dash is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, and the ads are limited to family-friendly content.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches the app.
